# NIC Government IT Services & Cloud Migration Advisory

Summary of official IT infrastructure and service transitions communicated by the National Informatics Centre (NIC) to user `rukmangadan.soi@gov.in`.

---

## 1. Overview of Transition: GovDrive to WorkDrive

The National Informatics Centre (NIC) notified all active government email users across `mail.gov.in` and `mail.nic.in` domains regarding the decommissioning of **GovDrive** and the mandatory adoption of **WorkDrive**.

### Why the Change?
Competent government authorities directed the modernization of cloud file-sharing and workplace collaboration infrastructure, standardizing on WorkDrive for government departments and officers.

---

## 2. Key Transition Timeline & Milestones

| Date | Milestone Event | User Action Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **31-Oct-2026** | **Upload Freeze on GovDrive** | GovDrive becomes read-only for uploads. Users must initiate all new file sharing via WorkDrive. |
| **01-Nov-2026** | **Automated File Migration Begins** | NIC backend begins transferring user files to associated WorkDrive accounts automatically. |
| **15-Dec-2026** | **Download Facility Cut-off** | Last day to manually verify and download legacy documents directly from GovDrive. |
| **31-Dec-2026** | **Complete Decommissioning** | The GovDrive portal is shut down permanently. |
